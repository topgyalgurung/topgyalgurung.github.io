Find me online

From the mountainside of Nepal to the heart of NYC, my journey has been shaped by curiosity, resilience and a deep love for technology. I've always been drawn to understanding how things work at a deeper level and find real fulfillment in building systems from the ground up.


As a full-stack developer, I craft scalable, user-focused solutions that blend logic, creativity, and impact—bringing ideas to life through code. I love working across the stack — from intuitive frontend with React and Next.js to clean, scalable backends with Node.js, Express, GraphQL, REST APIs and PostgreSQL. What drives me most is the challenge of turning ideas into real-world applications that solve problems and create value. I'm constantly learning, collaborating, and refining my craft — excited by what's possible and committed to building with purpose.
