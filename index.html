Flagship — live product

Himalayan Connect NYC

Live in production

The problem

The NYC Himalayan community's resources — housing leads, job postings, mutual-aid contacts — lived in scattered group chats and word of mouth. Nothing was searchable. Nothing persisted past whoever happened to be online that day.

My role

Sole engineer end to end, as a Springboard Software Engineering Fellow — translated Figma designs into a production application, then took it through hardening and deployment.

What I shipped

  • Authentication and role-based dashboards so different community roles see what's relevant to them
  • Search and filtering across community resources, plus interactive Google Maps integration for location-based listings
  • Redis caching layer that cut repeat-query response times through optimized data fetching
  • Rate limiting, firewall rules, and observability/speed-insight monitoring for production reliability
  • Unit and integration test coverage validating the above

Stack

Next.jsReactPostgreSQL RedisGoogle Maps APIFigma → Production

Process

Started from real Figma designs rather than a blank editor — every screen had to match an intended user flow before a line of logic went in, which kept the build honest about who it was actually for.

Outcome

Live today, giving the NYC Himalayan community one place to find housing, job, and mutual-aid resources instead of re-asking the same questions in a dozen group chats.
