Find me online

I'm a full-stack developer with a unique journey — from growing up in the countryside of Nepal to building a life in New York City. I've always been drawn to understanding how things work. In school, math and science were my strongest subjects, and problem-solving quickly became something I genuinely enjoyed.

That early interest evolved into a passion for technology and building. Though I entered the tech field a bit later than some, I dove in fully — from formal education to hands-on projects. I find real fulfillment in creating things from the ground up and learning through doing.

Today, I enjoy working across the stack — crafting intuitive frontends with React and Next.js, and designing scalable backends with Node.js, PostgreSQL, and Prisma. For me, software engineering is the ideal blend of logic, creativity, and real-world impact — and I'm excited to keep learning and building meaningful tools for people
